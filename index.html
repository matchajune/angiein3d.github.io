About Me

HI THERE I'M ANGIE

From Physical Space to 3D

With over 8 years of experience in spatial, architectural, and museum design, I built a solid foundation from which I evolved into a 3D Generalist.

For me, a digital environment is more than just geometry. My background in architecture gives me a deep understanding of scale, lighting, spatial layout, and flow—transforming every scene into a compelling narrative. I craft virtual environments, 3D animation, motion graphics, and video edits for projects ranging from commercial advertising to cultural and educational content.

SOFTWARE STACK & TOOLSET

Bridging technical architecture with digital 3D creation, my workflow leverages a comprehensive software ecosystem. On the architectural and spatial design side, I utilize AutoCAD, Revit, Navisworks, and Twinmotion for precise spatial modeling, BIM coordination, and real-time rendering. For digital environments, animation, and visual storytelling, I rely on Blender, Maya, and Nomad Sculpt for 3D modeling and sculpting, paired with After Effects and Adobe Creative Cloud for motion graphics and compositing. To complete the multimedia pipeline, I integrate Procreate for visual development and Adobe Audition for sound editing.

VERY FLEXIBLE WITH COMMUNICATIONS & LOCATIONS

Based in Mexico and open to remote work worldwide.

PASSION FOR 3D WORLDS

Building immersive environments and bringing digital spaces to life through 3D geometry and light. Crafting virtual worlds isn't just my profession—it's my passion. I constantly explore new techniques in Blender, Maya, and visual composition.
